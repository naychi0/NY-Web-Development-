Design guidelines

Design system

The concept, palette, type, sitemap, components and rules behind this 15-page corporate website.

1 · Design concept

Calm, credible, quietly Japanese-minimal

A premium food-manufacturer presence: generous white space, a single confident red, warm beige panels and charcoal for weight. Diagonal cut-outs echo the original Shwe Myanmar swoosh, giving the site a recognisable signature without decoration.

Content rules: nothing is invented. Anything unverified — history, certifications, ingredients, nutrition, contacts — is shown as a clearly marked placeholder.

7 · Guidelines

Rules for future development

  • Spacing: 4/8px scale; sections use --sec (56–104px); cards pad 22–28px.
  • Colour: red only for actions, accents and key numbers; charcoal for dark sections; beige for warm panels. Body text never lighter than #5C5852 on white.
  • Buttons: one red primary per view; ghost for secondary; minimum height 44–48px.
  • Imagery: real photography with natural colour, 4:3 or 16:9, 8px radius. Use placeholders until verified images exist.
  • Content integrity: never publish unverified certifications, ingredients, nutrition or history. Mark gaps with the Placeholder tag.
  • Accessibility: skip link, visible focus rings, semantic landmarks, alt text, keyboard-operable menus, reduced-motion support.
  • Brand: no third-party brand names, logos or imagery.
